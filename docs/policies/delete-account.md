# Deleting your Penroam Cloud account

This page is for **Penroam**, the handwriting and notes app, and its optional sync service,
Penroam Cloud. It explains how to delete your Penroam Cloud account and what happens to your data.

The Penroam app itself needs no account. If you never turned on Penroam Cloud sync, you have no
account to delete. Your notes are only on your devices: see [deleting your data](privacy.md#deleting-your-data).

## How to delete your account

### In the app

1. Open Penroam and go to **Settings → Sync**.
2. Under **Penroam Cloud**, choose **Delete account…**.
3. Read the message and choose **Continue in browser**.
4. Sign in again in your browser to confirm that it's you.

The account is scheduled for deletion straight away. **Settings → Sync** then shows the date it
will be erased.

### Without the app

Email **support@penroam.ink** from the email address you use to sign in to Penroam Cloud, with the
subject "Delete my account". We'll reply to that address to confirm the request before we schedule
the deletion. After that, it works the same as a request made in the app.

### Changing your mind

For **30 days** after the request, the account is kept but can't be changed. Sync stops uploading,
and you can still download and export your notebooks. To keep the account, choose **Keep my
account** in **Settings → Sync** before that date, or email us.

### Subscriptions

If you pay for Penroam Cloud through **Google Play** or the **App Store**, deleting your account
**doesn't cancel the subscription**. Cancel it yourself in the store's subscription settings, or
you'll keep being charged. A plan paid by card on a computer stops renewing when you ask for
deletion.

## What is deleted

30 days after your request, Penroam Cloud erases everything stored for your account:

* your synced notebooks, PDFs, images and other files, and their history (all stored encrypted)
* your vaults, their names and their locks
* your devices: their names and keys
* your account's encryption keys
* your sign-in sessions

Copies in the service's backups are overwritten within a further **35 days**. After that, none of
your notes or files remain anywhere in Penroam Cloud.

## What is kept

* **Notes on your devices.** Deleting the account never deletes notes on a phone, tablet or
  computer. Delete those in the app, or uninstall it and remove its data.
* **A record that the account existed.** It holds an account number and the ID of your sign-in
  (not your email address), so the same sign-in doesn't quietly start a fresh account. To have it
  removed, email us.
* **Security records.** Sign-ins, devices added and removed, and the deletion request, with dates
  and no note content.
* **Billing records.** Whether and how you paid, kept for as long as tax and accounting law
  requires. Your email address and payment details are held by our payment provider (Stripe), or
  by Google or Apple for store purchases, under their own policies. We never receive card
  numbers.
* **Sign-in.** Your email address and login history are held by our sign-in provider (Auth0), not by
  Penroam Cloud.
